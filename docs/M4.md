# M4: mining and fighting together

Goal: players in a cell can laser, grab, claw, load, bay and shoot any rock in it, take home exactly what they
docked, and see each other's ships, weapons, drones and deaths as they happen, with nothing duplicated, nothing
vanished and nothing written to anyone else's save. Design: [M4-OWNERSHIP.md](M4-OWNERSHIP.md) (rocks, damage,
ore, races) and [M4-SPAWN-AUDIT.md](M4-SPAWN-AUDIT.md) (every spawn site and player-owned system). Known issues
carried from M3 are at the end of [M3.md](M3.md).

Shared rules: inside an override of `_ready`, `_process`, `_physics_process`, `_enter_tree`, `_exit_tree`, `_input`
or `_init`, never call the parent version (Godot 3 already calls every level; `tools/verify.py` rejects it; a
differently named tick driver may call it). The packet names in M4-OWNERSHIP.md's table are the contract between tracks; change them only by
agreement. `python tools/verify.py` passes before every push. Game time goes through the lock (`DV_WHO`, `tools/stop.sh`).

## Order

1. **Own** starts with claim/release on a single held rock (claw) and the conservation log, because every other
   gate depends on ownership being right. **Ships** starts with puppet inertness and `s.die`, which don't need it.
2. Then **Own**: hold, MPU, bullets, beams. **Ships**: NPC mirroring, hostility.
3. Lead runs the gate on `m4` with both merged.

## Gate (all on `m4`, dev3 at `DIVE_X=150000`, zero SCRIPT ERROR throughout)

From M4-OWNERSHIP.md "Testing", plus the ships track:

1. Scripted claim race, 100 trials: exactly one owner every time, no rock lost or doubled.
2. Mid-fracture grab: the claw gets either the rock or the shards, never both, never neither.
3. Holder hard-kill: the held rock reappears in space for the others within 2 s.
4. Handoff while holding: the holder keeps the rock and the new authority learns it.
5. Beams: a laser on a rock owned by someone else damages it once, on the owner.
6. Bullets: mass driver on a rock owned by someone else, kinetic and thermal damage and push counted once.
7. Puppet inertness over 5 minutes: a puppet's claw, arm, drones and turrets never lock, push, fire or damage on
   their own; no puppet vanish; nothing written to the viewer's save.
8. Ship death: an owner's ship exploding is seen by everyone, the blast pushes the same rocks the same way, and the
   lifepod exists once, on the owner.
9. NPC mirror: a pirate spawned by the authority appears on members, is shot by a member, turns hostile to that member,
   and the members' saves are unchanged.
10. Ore audit: composition in (rocks) = composition out (everyone's docked cargo + live rocks) within rounding, logged
    by the conservation check over the run.

**Human gate:** an ore audit with real flying. Two people mine the same patch for 5 minutes, using claw, hold and
MPU, with one exit to Enceladus (Tim + one more pilot, or Tim flying two windows in turn). Dev automation covers
gates 1-9. A scripted claw/laser/mass-driver bot is part of the Own track's tooling.
